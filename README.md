# Buckshot Roulette VR
A fan-made, personal project that lets you play **Buckshot Roulette** in virtual reality. You sit (or stand) at the table in the
headset, look around the club, and use your own hands to do what the game normally asks you to do with a mouse.

> **AI-generated software - see the [AI disclaimer](#ai-disclaimer) below.**
>
> Not affiliated with the game's creator. For personal use only; the game's files are never copied or shared by this project.
> It is still being built and tested, so it is not packaged for other people yet.

## What you can do in VR
- **Look around and walk.** The bathroom, the lobby and the back room are all around you. Use the left thumbstick to walk and the
  right thumbstick to turn in 30-degree steps.
- **Point and click.** In the menus and once you are seated at the table, a laser from either controller replaces the mouse. Pull the trigger to click. The game's own rules still apply,
  so it plays exactly like the normal game. Before you sit down there is no laser at all: the doors, the TV, the pills, the name machine and the waiver are all done by hand. At the table the laser stays hidden too - everything is done by hand - and only appears when you aim at the two words you have to point at (the double-or-nothing YES / NO), and in the menus.
- **See your hands.** Your hands appear in the world and change shape as you point, grip or make a fist. They are lit by the game's
  own lights (an option in the pause menu).
- **The bathroom, by hand.** Poke the door and the TV with your finger, pick up the pill bottle, and bring it to your mouth to take
  the pills (or let go and it goes back on the desk).
- **Both doors: touch, then pull the trigger.** Touch the bathroom door or the back-room door with your finger (a lime frame lights up); pull the trigger and your leg kicks the door open
  right where you stand. Nothing moves you; you then walk through yourself. Touching alone does not open anything.
- **The TV's buttons are real buttons.** Push the X with your fingertip, straight in from the front: the button sinks under your finger and clicks near the bottom. That starts the TV; push it again to switch it off. The three other buttons on the TV work the same way.
  Touching the screen itself, or brushing a button from the side, does nothing.
- **Frames follow the thing.** The lime frame that shows what you can touch lies in the thing's own plane: flat ON the table (not under it) under an item, the shotgun or a slot; on the face of a door, a button or a latch; not turned toward your head. The little round markers are gone: the lime frames are the only highlight.
- **The gun in your hand looks like the gun on the table.** It is only lit a little so that it does not go black in front of your face; it no longer looks pale or glowing.
- **Sit down first.** The things on the table (waiver, items, shotgun) cannot be picked or highlighted until you have sat at the table.
- **Menus and text.** The game's menus and text float in front of you and can be clicked with the laser.
- **Make the world fit you.** The game starts at a comfortable size (50 %) and height (+15 cm). Open the pause menu (left menu button): four buttons make the world smaller or bigger (5 % per press) and your seat lower or higher (2 cm per press); the readout shows the values. The pause menu can also reset both.
  There is an optional two-hand gesture (switch it on in the pause menu: "Two-hand size"): hold both grips with empty hands, pull them apart / together for size, down / up for height. It is **off by default** and it never starts while a hand holds the shotgun, an item, a pump or the handcuffs.
- **The name machine, by hand.** Pick up the waiver with one hand (squeeze the grip next to it) and hold it up to your eyes to read it: the machine comes up. Open your hand and the paper falls away. Then touch the keys of the name machine with your fingertip. (If you do not pick the waiver up for 40 seconds, touching it with a finger works again.) When you press Enter with your name, the machine and the waiver are done for good: they cannot be touched any more, and a few seconds after the game has put them away they are hidden, so they can no longer disturb the game.
- **The shotgun, by hand - with both hands.** Squeeze a grip with your hand right at the shotgun to pick it up (if an item lies closer to your hand than the gun, you pick up the item instead): the game's own shotgun comes into your hand, with your hand wrapped around the narrow neck of its stock (the one on the table is hidden meanwhile).
  The game does not know you have it yet, so **your other hand is free**: it can take items from the table, use them on the gun in your hand (beer, magnifying glass, handsaw) and work the pump of the gun in your hand. While the other hand holds the pump, the barrel points along the line between your two hands, like a real two-hand hold. Open the hand that holds the gun over the place where it lay (a lime frame shows it) and it goes back on the table.
  Point it at the dealer, or hold it out toward your own face (the gun and your hand together slide away from you so the long gun fits in front of you): a short buzz in your hand tells you the aim is locked, and the trigger fires it (the game takes the gun in that moment; the bang follows a fraction of a second later).
  After the shot the game waits for you: a lime frame and a ring show where the gun goes on the table. Bring the gun to the frame (the ring turns lime) and open your hand; the gun settles on the table and the next turn begins. (If you do nothing, the game puts it down for you after 20 seconds.)
  Shooting is only possible when the game says it is your turn and no item is being used.
  **If the gun does not sit right in your hand:** while you hold it, click the thumbstick of the hand that holds it. A sign appears. Move the gun in your hand with that thumbstick (sideways, up and down) and with the other hand's thumbstick (in and out); B / Y on the other controller puts it back to the standard place. Click again to save: it is remembered for next time. Nothing can fire while you adjust.
- **Taking items out of the box.** When the game lets you take an item, put a hand into the box and squeeze the grip: the item comes out into your fist and stays in it (it is held, not just following). Carry it over the table (the nearest free slot is framed) and open your hand: it goes into that slot.
  The laser still works for this as well.
- **Using the items on the table, for real.** Squeeze the grip with your hand on an item to pick it up (it sits in your fist); a lime frame shows what you can pick up. There are no words and no round markers: the lime frames show what you can pick up and where it goes.
  - **Beer:** bring the can to your mouth and tilt it back for a moment. Then the shotgun has to be racked: grab its pump (a frame shows it) with either hand, pull it back all the way and push it forward. The shell flies out and lands on the floor. You can take the gun with your other hand while you hold the can, and drink with the gun in that hand.
  - **Magnifying glass:** when you pick it up a sign says BREAK THE GLASS; through the whole glass you see nothing special. Smash it down onto the table: it breaks into the game's own broken-glass shape. What is left of the glass is a window: hold it between your eye and the open shotgun (the shotgun lies on the table or is in your other hand) and the shell in the barrel shows up inside the window, big and turning. You can hold the gun in your other hand meanwhile. When you have looked (a buzz) and let go of the glass, it falls and fades away; if you keep holding it, it disappears by itself 5 seconds after it broke.
  - **Handsaw (cutter):** first flick the cutter open with a quick turn of your wrist (the blade swings out). Then hold it to the end of the barrel and saw back and forth. You hear the sawing while you saw; the cut-through and the sound of the tip dropping come only when the tip really falls off.
  - **Handcuffs:** hold them out toward the dealer; the ring fills; he reaches for them where you hold them and takes them.
  - **Cigarettes:** hold the pack and flick it open with a quick turn of your wrist (the lid flips up and a cigarette sticks out). Squeeze the grip of your OTHER hand at the cigarette to take it out of the pack (open that hand away from your face and it goes back). You can also hold the open pack itself up to your mouth, with the cigarette that sticks out going between your lips. Bring a cigarette's filter end to your mouth for a moment: you are healed at once, the empty pack falls away, and the cigarette sits in your mouth, glowing, with a little smoke, burning down in 10 seconds. A closed pack at your mouth does nothing.
  - **Inverter:** pick it up, then take its little dial with your other hand (squeeze at it) and turn it like a knob, a good half turn. It clicks while you turn; at the end the shell in the shotgun is flipped (live becomes blank and the other way round) and the inverter is used up. Let go early and the dial springs back.
  - **Expired medicine:** pick up the pack, pull the pill strip out with your other hand (squeeze at its end and pull, or just flick that hand), then squeeze at the pill or flick again: a pill drops into your palm. Bring your palm to your mouth to swallow it and see what it does. Open your hand away from your mouth and the pill goes back.
  - **Burner phone:** pick it up and flick your wrist: it flips open and a hint asks you to put it to your ear. Hold it at either ear for a moment and the phone's screen plays the game's own animation; then it tells you about one of the shells to come (the same message the game shows). Then it closes and is used up. Let go before the ear and it goes back on the table.
  - **Adrenaline:** pick up the syringe; a frame shows the spot on your other forearm. Jab the needle into it (a quick stab, or hold it there for a moment): the syringe stays stuck in your arm for a few seconds, and when the picture flares you are moved to the middle of the table. The dealer's items stay where they are: reach out and take one with your hand. You are back in your seat as soon as you pick one up, and you use it by hand like your own items. Put it back and you go to the table again until the time runs out.
  Open your hand anywhere else and the item goes back. You cannot pick items up when it is not your turn.
- **Handcuffed.** When the dealer cuffs you, your hands go into the two bracelets of the handcuffs that lie on the table (the game's own ones) and you hold them closed. You can move your hands a little around the bracelets; pull away and the cuffs turn on the table the way they do in the game. The controllers only buzz while you are actually pulling against the cuffs - a hand that rests in its place does not buzz. You cannot pick things up meanwhile.
- **Handing the handcuffs to the dealer.** Hold them out straight in front of you, about 40 cm from your head toward the dealer (a ring shows where). The dealer takes them there.
- **Blood when you are shot.** The old blood picture is gone. Like in other VR games, the edge of your view turns red (ragged, with a few drips running down), your controllers thump twice, and it ebbs away within a few seconds. The centre of your view stays clear.
- **The money briefcase.** At the end it slides in right in front of you, about 45 cm from your face, instead of far away in the middle of the table. Open its two latches by pushing them in with your fingertip, straight in from the front, about a centimetre (just touching them, or brushing them from the side, does nothing). Then take the lid with a squeeze of the grip and lift it about 10 cm.
- **A how-to sign in the bathroom.** At the start a short sign on the dark metal panel to the right of the bathroom door lists the
  controls. Point at its TAKE DOWN button and pull the trigger (or touch it) to remove it; the pause menu brings it back.
- **The game's colour look.** On the PC the game rounds every colour to a few levels, which gives its grainy, banded look. The headset now does the same
  (it can be turned off in the pause menu: "Retro colours"). The game's chunky low resolution is not copied: the headset picture stays sharp.
- **Your monitor shows what you see.** The PC window now shows a calm, level copy of your view (it follows where you look, smoothly, without tilting).
  It costs a little speed; the pause menu ("PC monitor view") or the F3 key turns it off.
- **The "god" letter.** When the game shows it at the first item grab, it floats up in front of you instead and the game waits: take it with one hand, read it, open your hand to go on.
- **A pause menu** (left menu button): it floats above your left hand, like a small panel you hold (half the old size, hidden by your other hand or the table when they are in front of it, never nearer than a reading distance); point at it with the other hand's laser. Styled like the game's own menus (its font, black and white with the game's green-yellow), with: recenter, comfort vignette, stick walking, floating text panel, item hints, hand lighting,
  size and height buttons, two-hand size on/off, reset size and height, bathroom sign, retro colours, PC monitor view, main menu, quit.

Still to come: smoke from the gun, touching the menus with your hands, more polish, and a simple installer.

## What you need
- A PC copy of Buckshot Roulette (Steam) and a headset that works with OpenXR. It is developed on a **Meta Quest 3 using Virtual
  Desktop**; other OpenXR headsets should work but have not been tried. Tested with game version 2.2.0.6.

## Install (the easy way)
1. On the **Releases** page of this project, download `Buckshot-Roulette-VR-<version>.zip`.
2. Open the game folder (Steam: right-click the game > Manage > Browse local files) and extract **everything** from the zip into it,
   so that `Buckshot Roulette VR.exe`, `override.cfg` and the `vr_mod` folder sit next to `Buckshot Roulette.exe`.
3. Connect your headset to the PC (Virtual Desktop, Quest Link or SteamVR as the OpenXR runtime), then double-click
   **Buckshot Roulette VR.exe**. It starts the game in VR.

To play without VR, start the game from Steam as usual (or run `Buckshot Roulette VR.exe --flat`): the mod stays idle.
To uninstall, delete those three items from the game folder. The `.exe` is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show
"unknown publisher" (More info > Run anyway); the zip is built from this repository by GitHub Actions and has a SHA-256 file.

### Manual setup (for developers)
Copy `vr_boot.gd` and the `src` folder into a folder `vr_mod` in the game folder, put `release/override.cfg` next to the game's exe
(or use `user://vr_mod/...` in the autoload line to keep the code in `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Buckshot Roulette\vr_mod\`),
and start the game with `--xr-mode on` from the game folder: `& ".\Buckshot Roulette.exe" --xr-mode on`. `python3 tools/make_release.py --version X`
builds the zip.

## Controls
| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Trigger (either hand) | Click. That hand becomes the pointer |
| Grip | Grab the pill bottle; the hand points |
| A / X | Accept |
| B / Y | Cancel (closes the TV or the pause menu) |
| Left stick | Walk in the building; menu arrows elsewhere |
| Right stick | Turn left / right in 30-degree steps |
| Left stick click | Recenter (sit comfortably, look at the table, press); while you hold the shotgun with the left hand: start / save the gun adjustment |
| Right stick click | Show / hide the floating text panel (while you hold the shotgun: start / save the gun adjustment) |
| Left menu button | Pause menu |
| Both grips, held | (Optional, off by default - switch it on in the pause menu) World size (hands apart / together) and height (hands down / up); never while a hand holds something |

## AI disclaimer
This mod was written almost entirely by an AI model (Claude, made by Anthropic), working as a coding agent under the direction of the project's owner. The owner decides what the mod should do, plays it in a real headset (Quest 3 over Virtual Desktop) and reports what is wrong; the AI writes and changes the code, runs automated offline checks that do not need a headset, and keeps these notes.

What that means for you:
- The code has **not** been written, reviewed or audited line by line by a human, and it has only been tested by one person on one setup (Steam version 2.2.0.6 of the game, Godot 4.1.1, OpenXR). Expect bugs and rough edges.
- It is provided **as is, without any warranty**. Use it at your own risk; back up your game folder and your saves before trying it.
- It comes with **no game files**: you need your own legal copy of Buckshot Roulette. It is a fan-made personal project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's creator or by Anthropic.
- A game update can break it at any time.

## For developers
The code is split into feature modules so you can work on one thing at a time: `src/README.md` has the table (gun, items, hands, brackets, pause menu, ...) and the few rules of the chain; `tools/check_modules.py` checks them; `tests/` runs the logic against mock game objects in a headless Godot (no headset needed). `ARCHITECTURE.md` explains how the mod and the game fit together.
